Contract controls

Privileged roles and supply authority

DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE

Held by 0x1fe8…08f0. This role can grant and revoke the bridge role.

BRIDGE_ROLE

Also held by 0x1fe8…08f0. It can mint without exceeding the 250 million cap and burn tokens for which it has an allowance.

Current supply effect

The full 250 million cap is currently minted, leaving no present minting headroom. Supply could be re-minted after a burn, but total supply cannot exceed the fixed cap.

Governance status

The bridge function was built to prepare TAOT for future cross-chain deployment, creating a path for the token to expand beyond Base while preserving its fixed 250 million maximum supply across supported networks. As that interoperability strategy develops, the project will document the bridge architecture and evaluate moving privileged roles to an appropriate multisig or dedicated bridge setup. Any completed role changes and supporting on-chain transactions will be published here.
