Android app live on Google Play

TAOT Android App

Take control of your crypto with the TAOT App, a powerful self-custody wallet built for the Base network. Securely store, send, receive, and swap TAOT (The America Only Token), USDC, and ETH directly from one streamlined app while keeping control of your own wallet and keys.

TAOT uses an 18-word recovery phrase, giving your wallet more recovery-phrase entropy than traditional 12-word configurations, and adds another layer of protection with Android biometrics or PIN access. Sensitive key material is protected using the Android Keystore, helping keep your wallet credentials secured on your device.

With built-in token swapping and a simple, intuitive interface, TAOT combines convenience, security, and true self-custody in one Base-native wallet. Your keys. Your crypto. Your control.

iOS app in progress

TAOT iOS App

The TAOT iOS app has been submitted to Apple and is currently awaiting App Store review. Once approved, it will bring the same Base-native self-custody wallet experience to Apple users, including TAOT, USDC, and ETH storage, sending, receiving, and built-in token swapping.

The iOS release is planned to include recovery-phrase wallet control, a streamlined interface, official TAOT resources, podcast content, and token information in one mobile app. App Store review timing is controlled by Apple and may take time to complete.
